DOPPEL

Reconstruct what each winning operator saw at the moment they traded. Learn the rule that turned it into orders, prove that rule fires on its own on held-out data, then run it on Polymarket US and Kalshi under one risk system that retires it when the edge decays.

Verdict

The idea is right. The earlier builds copied the wrong thing.

Every prior attempt that followed wallets lost money: a copy 5 seconds late earned +0.7% [−2.0, +3.6], the top 20% of leaders copied forward earned −4.2%, and RN1 single-leg copies ran −8% to −21% under strict replay. Every attempt that identified the mechanism found something real. Latency snipers' fills followed a BTC move in the same direction 89–93% of the time. A LoL taker had 98% of fills within 60 s of a game event. Complete-set makers locked 2–4¢ per pair plus rebates.

So DOPPEL never copies a fill. It clones the policy: the trigger, the price, the size, both legs, and the recovery. Wallet identities are training labels, never triggers.

The reframe

Why "same exact time" means cloning

A wallet's identity first appears on Polygon, at least one block after the price has already moved. No amount of Rust makes a copy faster than that. To trade alongside them, observe what they observed and run the same rule.

t0Their signal arrives: spot tick, goal, kill, newsthe thing to clone
t1Order hits the CLOB~ms after t0
t2Trade on the WebSocket, price and size, no identity~100 ms
t3Fill settles on-chain with identity, role, fee and order hash≥1 block · ~5 s finality
t4Indexers and the data-api show itseconds to ~100 s
Question 1 · skillIs it real?Point-in-time persistence against matched controls on the exact ledger.
Question 2 · clonabilityCan public state explain it?Fit π̂(action | state at t0−δ). The held-out lift over the wallet's base rate is the explainability ceiling.
Question 3 · economicsDoes our clone make money alone?Run π̂ on held-out US-venue tape in the twin, net of fees, queue and latency. Our trades, not theirs.

The anticipation test is the strongest proof. On held-out weeks, our clone fires before the wallet's on-chain fill, on the same side, at a preregistered rate. That measures "trading like them at the same time" directly.

Architecture

Two planes, one sealed bridge

The research plane can be messy, restartable and LLM-assisted. The execution plane only ever receives a sealed, hashed strategy that passed the foundry. Agents never touch keys, orders or caps.

Persona agents

Eight analysts with narrow jobs and no keys

They run in nightly batches over sealed dossiers, with a hard monthly token cap logged per agent. A claim without a graph ID is dropped. Each agent is scored by the share of its proposals that survive the gates. They arrive in milestone 5, after the pipeline they feed exists.

Learning loops

Five loops, each closing on evidence

  1. Atlas, weekly. Re-rank point-in-time, update the census, queue new operators for profiling.
  2. Inversion, per operator. Fit the policy, measure explainability and anticipation, send clonable ones to the foundry.
  3. Execution, daily. Our fills plus target makers' fills recalibrate the queue model and score twin fidelity.
  4. Allocation, daily. Capacity-capped Thompson or fractional-Kelly across promoted strategies, gated on calibration.
  5. Decay, continuous. Sentinel demotes; the Coroner records why; the failure library reshapes what gets proposed next. If the original operator's edge fades or they stop trading, the clone's prior drops before our own P&L shows it.

Build order

Size the prize before building the swarm

M1 · ~2 wks

Census. 120 days of Goldsky v2 fills into Parquet; Quinn's ingester audits 20 random spans. Ledger and G0. Point-in-time atlas, operator clusters, and a mechanism census: net dollars by mechanism × category × horizon, the clonable share, and the share with a liquid US target. Start a PM US 5m order-book recorder on day one so M2 has tape.

Program kill line. If the census finds less than a preset amount of clonable, US-transferable net profit (suggested $500/day), DOPPEL stops at M1 and the census feeds SHADOWBOOK as a toxicity map. That outcome costs two weeks, not two months.
M2

First clone: 5m/15m crypto latency taker. Highest prior evidence, and the sensors exist (PERELMAN BRTI, spot ticks). Inversion, anticipation, twin on PM US 5m tape, gates G2 to G4.

M3

5m complete-set maker. Needs the queue model and reuses Quinn's maker-pair preregistration. Check the rebate-tier economics first, since the rebate is 0% below $200k of 30-day maker volume.

M4

Book, Sentinel and Desk in Edge-engine strategies/clone. Shadow paper to G5.

M5

Persona agents and gene recombination. Then in-play esports and tennis, after G3 says whether a paid low-latency feed is needed.

Expectations

What success looks like

The best operators earned roughly $400–2,000 a day each, with median trades of $4–10. Capacity is small and contested. The upside is many small clones across mechanisms and markets, not one big one. Some of the best wallets will turn out unclonable. That is still a usable result, because they become the list of flow your makers should not quote against.
