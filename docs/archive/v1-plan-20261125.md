# DOPPEL — Polymarket strategy-inversion system

*Research + architecture + game plan · 2026-11-25 · paper/research only; live orders stay behind `riskd` and owner arming*

> **One line:** DOPPEL does not copy wallets. It reconstructs the information each winning operator saw at
> each decision, learns the *policy* that turned that information into orders, proves the policy fires
> on its own (before or with the original) on held-out data, and then runs that policy on US-legal
> venues — Polymarket US and Kalshi — under one risk system that retires it when its edge decays.

## 0. Verdict first

The idea is right, and you have already proven half of it the hard way. Every prior attempt that
**followed** wallets lost (copy at +5 s: +0.7 % [−2.0, +3.6]; top-20 % forward: −4.2 %; RN1 single-leg
stress replay: −8 to −21 %). Every attempt that **identified the mechanism** found something real:
latency snipers whose fills follow a BTC move 89–93 % of the time; LoL takers with 98 % of fills
within 60 s of a game event; complete-set makers locking 2–4 ¢/pair plus rebates. What failed
was not the concept. It was five specific build mistakes (§2), three parallel half-systems, and a
7 GB box running 19 services.

DOPPEL fixes that with one pipeline: **chain-exact ledger → point-in-time atlas → policy inversion →
twin replay → foundry gates → paper → riskd live → decay sentinel**, plus persona agents that
*propose* and deterministic evaluators that *decide*.

Two facts from this week change the economics:

1. **Polymarket US now lists 5-minute BTC/ETH/SOL/XRP up/down markets** (launched ~Oct 27, 2026). The
   most profitable bot archetype in your Oct-16 hunt (5m crypto makers and latency takers, Sharpe 10–21)
   was untransferable before, because PM US had no 5m. It is now transferable *in principle*.
2. **Goldsky's `polymarket.order_filled` v2 dataset** carries `order_type` (maker/taker), `order_hash`,
   `counterparty_id`, `fee`, and `builder` per fill, with historical backfill. That replaces the
   self-run archive Quinn was building. At the pilot's measured density (64 blocks → 13 MiB SQLite),
   that archive would be **~8.8 GiB/day** (~790 GiB per 90 days), which Apex (121 GB free, 7 GB RAM)
   cannot hold.

## 3. The reframe: inversion, not imitation

### 3.1 The two clocks (why "same exact time" means *cloning*)

```
t0  wallet's signal arrives (spot tick, goal, kill, news)            ← the thing to clone
t1  wallet's order hits the CLOB (off-chain)                         ~ms after t0
t2  match broadcast on CLOB WebSocket — price/size, NO identity      ~100 ms
t3  fill settles on Polygon — WITH identity, role, fee, order_hash   ≥ 1 block (~2 s), finality ~5 s
t4  data-api / indexers show it                                      seconds to ~100 s
```

Identity only exists at **t3**, at least one block after the price has already moved. No amount of
Rust makes a copy faster than t3. The only way to trade "at the same time" is to observe what they
observed at **t0** and run the same rule. Identities are **labels for training**, not triggers.

### 3.2 Three questions per operator

For every operator (wallet *cluster*, see §5.3) DOPPEL answers three questions in order. Each one gates the next.

1. **Is the skill real?** Point-in-time persistence vs. matched controls on the exact ledger (L1, L2).
2. **Can it be explained from public state?** Fit π̂(action | state at t0 − δ). The **explainability
   ceiling** is the held-out lift of π̂ over the wallet's base rate (AUC / log-loss gain on side,
   timing hazard, size). High lift means clonable. Low lift means private info or model, so the
   operator becomes a toxicity label (F6).
3. **Does our clone make money on its own?** Run π̂ independently on held-out tape in the twin. Score
   its trades (not the wallet's) net of fees, queue, and latency, on the **target US venue**.

**The anticipation test** is the strongest proof. On held-out data, our clone fires *before* the
wallet's on-chain fill, on the same side, at least X % of the time. That is "trading like them at the
same time", measured.

**The one-way bridge is the load-bearing design choice.** The research plane can be messy, Python,
LLM-assisted, and restartable. The execution plane only ever receives a **sealed, hashed
StrategySpec** that passed the foundry. Agents never touch keys, orders, or caps. That is the
existing Edge-engine rule (`riskd` sole key holder, research cannot raise caps), kept as is.

### L0 Ingest (truth sources)

- **Historical + identity:** Goldsky `order_filled` v2 (maker/taker, order_hash, counterparty, fee,
  builder) + `user_positions`/`user_balances`, via Turbo pipeline to Parquet. Cross-check random block
  ranges against our own `eth_getLogs` (Quinn's ingester becomes the **auditor**, not the archive).
  Known risk: public HF dumps have 9–60 h gaps, and Polymarket **Protocol V2 markets launched
  Nov 23, 2026 with different position IDs**. The decoder must cover CTF Exchange V2, NegRisk, and the
  new V2 market system. Every span gets a completeness receipt (block continuity + count vs a second source).
- **Live identity tail:** own Polygon log subscription (finalized blocks) → same schema, ≤10 s behind.
- **Anonymous fast tape:** CLOB WebSocket book + trades on a *declared universe* (not just wallet
  markets). This is the only way to see resting quotes, cancels, and queue. It is joined to chain fills
  by (token, price, size, time window) and labeled with a match confidence.
- **Sensors (t0 reconstruction):** CF/BRTI replica (PERELMAN), Binance/Coinbase ticks, Chainlink
  where it settles, sports/esports state feeds, rules text and fee snapshots. All carry
  event/publish/receipt clocks (Quinn's three-clock rule).

### L1 Ledger (the moat everyone else gets wrong)

Per wallet × market: fills (role, price, fee) + split/merge (complete-set mint/burn) + redemptions +
rebates/rewards + transfers + open marks at executable bid depth. Exchange-internal splits are not
counted as wallet profit. **Gate G0:** ≥95 % of sampled wallets reconcile to `v2/user-pnl`
economic_pnl within max($50, 2 %). Until G0 passes, nothing downstream runs.

P&L is then **decomposed by mechanism**: spread capture (vs contemporaneous mid), directional value
(mark-to-settlement), complete-set lock, rebates/rewards, and fees. This decomposition *is* the
strategy fingerprint.

### L2 Atlas

- **Point-in-time skill:** at each weekly ranking date, score with data known then, freeze, and
  measure on the next 4 weeks of disjoint events vs activity/price-band/category-matched controls.
  Event-clustered intervals, largest-winner exclusion.
- **Operator clustering:** wallets → operators via co-quoting (shared tx), order-hash/builder
  patterns, funding-transfer graph, synchronized cancels. Bosona/almach/mo-money already looked like
  one operator. **The unit of strategy is the operator, not the wallet.**
- **Mechanism census (first deliverable):** where the money on Polymarket goes, by mechanism ×
  category × horizon, net of fees, with the share that is (a) clonable and (b) transferable to a US
  venue with liquidity. This sizes the prize before anything else is built.
- **Counterparty map:** who systematically loses to whom (from `counterparty_id`). It yields the
  "fade/avoid" list for maker skew (identity-aware MM from SHADOWBOOK).

### L3 Knowledge graph

Keep Quinn's SQLite graph pattern (identities, time, hashes in the graph; streams in Parquet archives
with offsets). Node types: Wallet, Operator, Market/Condition/Token, RuleVersion, FeeVersion, Fill,
LifecycleEvent, SensorObservation, Mechanism, Hypothesis, Genome, Trial, Verdict, Deployment.
Edges carry **valid-time and knowledge-time** (bitemporal), so any feature can be recomputed exactly
as of a past decision. Lineage follows W3C PROV (source → transform → feature → decision → order →
payout). A rule/fee/mapping change invalidates its dependents. Start with DuckDB over Parquet plus
SQLite for edges. Move to a graph DB only if traversal queries become the bottleneck (they won't at this scale).

### L4 Inversion lab (the frontier core)

1. **State reconstruction at t0−δ:** for each labeled action, rebuild the full public state the
   operator *could* have had: book (both tokens), last trades, sensor values, time-to-expiry, rule
   regime, and their own inventory (from the ledger). δ sweeps 0–10 s to find the information horizon.
2. **Policy models, simplest first:**
   - trigger hazard model (when they act): point process / survival on state features
   - side and price-level model (what): gradient-boosted classifier, then calibrated
   - size and inventory policy (how much): quantile regression on inventory + edge
   - for makers: **inverse quoting model**, which infers quote placement from fills + L2 survival
   - Sequence models (temporal transformer over event streams) are admitted only if they beat the
     tabular baseline on sealed holdouts by a preregistered margin.
3. **Explainability ceiling + information horizon** per operator → clonability score.
4. **Anticipation test:** clone fires before the chain fill on the same side ≥ preregistered rate on
   held-out weeks.
5. **Trigger discovery without wallets:** once a mechanism is identified, search for its trigger
   across *all* markets (not just where the wallet traded). This is where capacity comes from.

### L5 Foundry: strategy genome + registry

A strategy is a typed genome (extends Edge-engine StrategySpec v2): `Universe × Trigger ×
FairValue × Execution(maker/taker/pair/recovery) × Sizing × Exit × RiskEnvelope`. A clone decomposes into
genes. "Assemble parts" means recombining genes (operator A's trigger, operator B's execution style,
our risk envelope). **Every recombination is charged to the FDR ledger**, which keeps search from
manufacturing a winner. Preregistration hashes the genome, data windows, metric, power,
futility bound, and kill rule before the holdout is opened.

### L6 Twin

One event-driven replayer for research and paper (Edge-engine's paper broker semantics): post-arrival
books only; a print cannot fill the quote it caused; maker fills need queue-ahead depletion by later
aggressor volume (same-token sells **and** complementary-token buys ≥ 1−bid, deduped by match id);
latency drawn from *measured* per-venue distributions; fee curves pinned per venue and version;
conservative/base/stressed arms. **Twin fidelity gate:** twin-predicted vs actual fills on our own
paper/live probes, and on the target wallets' observed maker fills.

### L7 Book + L8 Sentinel

- `strategies/clone` desk in Edge-engine consumes sealed genomes. Paper and live share one code path.
  Only `riskd` signs.
- **Sentinel:** per-strategy live markouts (1 s/10 s/60 s/settle), realized vs twin-forecast edge,
  **anytime-valid e-value** tests on "edge > hurdle" (valid under continuous monitoring), and
  CUSUM/Bayesian-online-changepoint on edge per trade. Demotion is automatic (live → paper → retired)
  when the e-process for "edge ≤ 0" crosses its threshold or capacity collapses. A promotion needs
  the owner. A demotion never does.
- **Champion/challenger swap:** challengers run in shadow on the same events; capital moves only on
  a preregistered paired contrast.
- **Source-decay early warning:** if the *original operator's* edge (from the atlas) decays,
  or they stop trading, the clone's prior is down-weighted before our own P&L shows it.

## 5. Persona agents (LLM proposes, code decides)

All agents run **batch** (nightly / on dossier change), read sealed dossiers, write proposals into
the registry, and have **no** order, key, or cap access. Budget: hard monthly token cap, logged per
agent. Model routing: Haiku for extraction/triage, Sonnet for dossiers, Opus only for the weekly
Strategist/Skeptic round.

| Persona | Job | Output | Can't |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cartographer** | Coverage + completeness: gaps, decoder versions, new market systems | coverage report, gap tickets | mark data complete (receipts do) |
| **Accountant** | Investigates G0 reconciliation failures; explains residuals | reconciliation notes | change the ledger rules |
| **Profiler** | Writes an operator dossier: mechanism decomposition, timing, venues, cluster | dossier (cited to graph IDs) | assert skill (atlas does) |
| **Detective** | Proposes the t0 trigger and the sensors needed; designs the state features | hypothesis + feature spec | fit or grade models |
| **Strategist** | Drafts genomes, recombinations, US-venue transfer plan | preregistration draft | register it (human/CI seal) |
| **Skeptic (red team)** | Argues luck, look-ahead, rebates, private info, survivorship, crowding; proposes falsifiers | falsifier list attached to the trial | veto (evaluator decides) |
| **Quartermaster** | Execution feasibility: latency budget vs measured, depth, capacity, fee tier | feasibility card | mark feasible without measurements |
| **Coroner** | Post-mortem on every retired strategy: failure cause → failure library | failure library entry | — |

Claims without graph/evidence IDs are dropped automatically (the edgestack AI-desk rule). Agents are
judged like strategies: the fraction of their proposals that survive the gates.

## 6. Learning loops

1. **Atlas loop (weekly):** re-rank PIT → census delta → new operators/mechanisms → Profiler queue.
2. **Inversion loop (per operator):** fit π̂ → explainability/anticipation → clonable? → foundry.
3. **Execution loop (daily):** our paper/live fills + target wallets' maker fills → recalibrate queue
   and fill model → twin fidelity score.
4. **Allocation loop (daily):** capacity-capped Thompson/Kelly-fraction allocation across promoted
   strategies, gated on calibration (APEX doctrine: aggression must be earned by prospective evidence).
5. **Decay loop (continuous):** Sentinel e-values/changepoints → demote/swap → Coroner → failure
   library → Detective/Strategist priors (what died and why changes what gets proposed next).

## 8. Build order

**Milestone 1 — "Census" (≈ 2 weeks; sizes the prize; no agents, no execution).**
1. Goldsky `order_filled` v2 + positions → Parquet, 120 days, all categories. Quinn ingester audits 20 random spans.
2. Ledger + G0 reconciliation on a stratified 200-wallet sample.
3. PIT atlas + operator clustering + **mechanism census**: $ by mechanism × category × horizon, the
   clonable share, and the share transferable to PM US/Kalshi with measured US depth.
4. **Preregistered kill criterion for the whole program:** if the census finds < $X/day (owner sets X;
   suggested $500/day) of net profit in mechanisms that are clonable *and* have a US-liquid target,
   DOPPEL stops at M1 and the census becomes a SHADOWBOOK toxicity input. That is still a useful
   result, and it costs two weeks instead of two months.

**Milestone 2 — "First clone": the 5m/15m crypto latency taker** (highest prior evidence, sensors
already built: PERELMAN BRTI, Binance/Coinbase ticks; PM US 5m now exists). Inversion → anticipation →
twin on PM US 5m tape (start a PM US 5m L2 recorder on day 1 of M1 so the tape exists) → G2–G4.

**Milestone 3 — the 5m complete-set maker** (needs the queue model; uses the maker-pair prereg code
from Quinn). Check rebate-tier economics first: below $200k/30 d maker volume the rebate is 0 %.

**Milestone 4 — Book + Sentinel + Desk** in Edge-engine (`strategies/clone`), shadow paper → G5.

**Milestone 5 — persona agents + recombination**, after there is a working pipeline to feed. Then
in-play esports/tennis (needs a paid low-latency feed, G3).

Agents arrive in M5 on purpose. A swarm with no ledger, twin, or gates is the failure mode of F7.
