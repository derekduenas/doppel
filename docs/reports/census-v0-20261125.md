## Anticipation (taker fills vs raw BRTI, BTC)
Most active takers REACT_TO_PUBLIC_MOVE (incl. dangerously-skip-permissions, Bonereaper, bosona): their taker fills
follow raw-BRTI moves of the prior 1–5 s more than placebo. Reacting to a public move is not the same as profiting
from it: the late-taker class is net negative.

## Caveats (read before acting)
- 1–6 snapshot intervals per wallet: rankings are noisy; NO persistence (point-in-time skill) has been measured yet.
  That needs cohort v1 scored on days after its freeze.
- Rebates/rewards come from the whole-wallet API, attributed to window P&L (fine for window specialists, overstated for
  mixed wallets).
- "locked" uses average buy prices of both sides per window; negative values mean pairs bought above $1 (directional).
- ETH/SOL/XRP have no raw index here, so fair-value features are BTC-only.
