DOPPEL Architecture

Venues feed one append-only tape. The research plane turns it into exact P&L, who earns and how, fair value, flow intelligence, and finally executable strategy genomes that a replay twin scores. Only a sealed genome crosses into execution, where a single key-holder sends Polymarket US quotes and Kalshi hedges. Results flow back into a lineage graph and the learning loops.

How to read it
Data path

Six sensors write raw messages to one append-only tape on Apex. Research reads it as-of, so a decision only sees what had arrived by then. Chain data from Goldsky reconciles every wallet's P&L to Polymarket's own numbers before anything is believed.

Strategy path

Census finds who earns, dossiers show how, and policy inversion recovers the rule. Extraction writes the rule as a genome, and the registry seals it before its test window exists. The twin replays Polymarket US books and prints to decide PASS or KILL.

Money path

Only a sealed genome reaches execution. The same genome runs on paper and live. riskd enforces caps and the kill switch. Maker quotes go to Polymarket US, and inventory is hedged on Kalshi's identical 15-minute contract.

Invariants the architecture enforces
  • As-of only. Every feature uses data received by the decision second. Venue history endpoints that look ahead are banned (Polymarket US price history, Polymarket US REST books).
  • Truth before belief. Wallet P&L must reconcile to Polymarket's own numbers (G0) and census figures must pass their self-check before they're used.
  • Preregistration. A genome's test window must lie in the future when it is sealed, and every variant counts against its family's false-discovery budget.
  • Agents propose, code decides. LLM agents draft dossiers and genomes; deterministic gates issue verdicts.
  • One key holder. Only riskd signs orders. Polymarket US market data is signed by an existing signer, and DOPPEL never holds that key.
  • Owner arms live. Nothing trades real money until you arm it, inside riskd's caps.
