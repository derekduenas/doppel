The one-of-one edge

A public, role-labeled tape of the deepest market, pointed at the venues it can't reach

Polymarket international publishes every fill with the wallet, its maker or taker role, and the fee, about two seconds after the trade prints. That was measured live today. US persons can't trade there, so the operators trading there mostly can't trade Polymarket US or Kalshi. Those venues list the same 5-minute and 15-minute crypto windows and the same sports.

v1 asked which wallet to imitate. v2 asks what the entire intl market knows two seconds after it knows it, and where that knowledge isn't yet in a US price.

Capability 1Flow AtlasExact ledger of the whole intl market, clustered into operators and scored point-in-time. Every live fill gets labeled informed, neutral or retail within ~2 s.
Capability 2Policy inversionFor skilled operators that public data can explain, rebuild what they saw and fire the same trigger. Unexplainable ones become toxicity labels.
Capability 35m complex engineOne fair-value engine for the same BTC/ETH/SOL/XRP windows across three venues, three settlement indexes and three fee curves. It quotes on the US venues.

Profit hypotheses

Ranked by distance to the first dollar

#HypothesisVenueWhat kills it
H1Guarded US maker. Quote PM US 5m/15m around a fair value from the index replica, the intl mid and intl informed flow. Pull or widen when labeled informed operators hit intl in the same window.PM US, Kalshi 15mNegative markouts after the guard; queue too long; US already tracks intl inside our latency
H2Cross-venue lead. Intl moves first and US books lag. Re-quote faster, or take where edge beats the fee near 0 or 1.PM US, KalshiLag shorter than our tick-to-trade
H3Latency-taker clone. Invert the intl snipers and fire the same spot trigger on US windows.Kalshi 15m, PM US extremesSensor parity fails; fees
H4Endgame and structural. Near-certain last minutes; ladder consistency.PM US, KalshiThin margin, tail risk
H5In-play clones for esports, tennis and soccer.PM US sportsNeeds a paid fast event feed

Illustrative capacity, not a forecast: ~180k shares per BTC 5m window × 288 windows a day is on the order of 50M shares/day on PM US. A maker that captured 0.5¢ per share on 2% of that would earn about $5k/day before adverse selection. M1 and M2 exist to replace this arithmetic with measured markouts.

Plan

Six milestones, each with a test that ends it

M0 · days 1–3

Recorders. Intl books, prints and RTDS; PM US and Kalshi windows; spot and index ticks. Done when 24 h of tape shows ≥99.5% hourly completeness and the watchdog drill passes. It comes first because order books can't be backfilled.

M1 · wks 1–2

Census. 120-day Goldsky backfill, ledger with G0, atlas, operators, mechanism census.

Program kill line. If clonable, US-transferable net profit comes in under your line (suggested $500/day) and there is no measurable intl→US lead, DOPPEL stops and keeps the atlas.
M2 · wks 2–4

Complex and H1 twin. Calibrated fair value per venue, live labels, the twin with ported queue models. Done when H1 passes G4 on at least two weeks of PM US tape.

M3 · wks 4–6

Shadow and riskd. Runtime, riskd and sentinel run paper on live data, with desk v1. Passing G5 (≥14 days, e ≥ 20) leads to your decision on micro-live with $25–100 caps.

M4 · wks 5–8

Inversion to H3. Top atlas operators, anticipation tests, H2 and H3 genomes through G2–G4.

M5 · wks 8+

Simulator, agents, H5. Learned multi-agent simulator, batch agents, then sports and esports once G3 passes.
