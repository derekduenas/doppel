# DOPPEL

DOPPEL learns who is right on Polymarket international and why, then makes markets on Polymarket US and Kalshi using that knowledge.

- Design and plan: [docs/BLUEPRINT.md](docs/BLUEPRINT.md)
- Measured facts behind it: [evidence/2026-11-25-probes/FINDINGS.md](evidence/2026-11-25-probes/FINDINGS.md)
- Open-source and ported references: [third_party/REFERENCES.md](third_party/REFERENCES.md)

Status: design and probes only. No services are running. Live trading requires explicit owner arming through `riskd`. Polymarket international is used as read-only public data; execution targets are US venues only.
